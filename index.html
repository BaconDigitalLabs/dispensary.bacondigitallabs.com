What a platform build covers

Whether you're launching a new storefront or fixing one that's fighting your team, the build starts with your existing catalog and systems—custom code only where the platform genuinely can't do the job.

In scope

  • New storefront build or migration on Dutchie, Jane, or Meadow
  • Theming and branding to match your store, not the platform default
  • Catalog, category, and menu structure set up for how customers actually shop
  • Checkout, pickup, and delivery flow configured and tested
  • Integration with your POS and inventory system
  • Age-gating and advertising configuration reviewed against WSLCB rules
  • Optional ancillary storefront (Shopify/WooCommerce) for non-plant-touching merchandise
  • Documented handoff: access inventory, change log, and a plain-English runbook

What you get

  • A storefront your own team can update without waiting on a developer
  • One clear owner for catalog, pricing, and promo changes
  • A build reviewed for common WSLCB advertising and age-restriction pitfalls
  • Fewer "why is the menu wrong" fire drills
  • A documented path to ongoing care once you're live
  • Direct access to who built it—not a support ticket queue
